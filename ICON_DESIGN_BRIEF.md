# Desktop App Icon Design Brief

## Product context

Working product name: **Trade-log Analytics**.

This app helps a trading team run local Python backtests, compare strategy
parameter combinations, and review performance, drawdown, and trade history.
It uses saved market data and is intended to work offline. Team distribution
as an installed desktop application is planned; packaging is not complete yet.

The icon should represent rigorous testing and analysis. It should not suggest
that the app places live orders or guarantees trading profits.

## Design objective

Create a distinctive desktop app icon that feels precise, calm, and dependable.
It must be recognizable on a Windows taskbar, desktop shortcut, Start menu,
and installer, including at small sizes and against light or dark backgrounds.

## Suggested concepts to explore

Develop three distinct concepts before selecting a final direction:

1. **Structured ledger:** a compact geometric mark based on aligned records or
   columns, with one contrasting element representing a selected result.
2. **Test and compare:** a few simplified paths or blocks that express comparing
   strategy variations. Keep the silhouette simple enough for a taskbar icon.
3. **Custom monogram:** a distinctive T or TL mark incorporating a subtle
   analytical motif through negative space.

These are exploration directions, not approved designs. The mark should remain
recognizable without the product name beside it.

## Visual character

- Strong silhouette and simple geometry.
- Restrained detail, balanced spacing, and clear contrast.
- Use the existing brand's dark charcoal, warm coral, and off-white as a
  starting palette; consult DESIGN.md for the application color tokens.
- Keep color choices limited and ensure the mark also works in monochrome.
- Explore a contained tile and a standalone symbol before choosing a shape.

## Avoid

- Generic upward stock arrows, currency symbols, bulls, bears, or rockets.
- Dense candlestick charts, tiny labels, and miniature dashboard screenshots.
- Neon trading-terminal aesthetics or excessive glow and glossy effects.
- Details that disappear at small sizes.
- Dependence on red versus green to communicate meaning.
- Copying another trading application's mark.

## Requested deliverables

- Three initial concepts shown side by side, each with a short rationale.
- A final editable vector master, preferably SVG.
- Transparent PNG exports at 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256, 512, and 1024 pixels.
- A Windows ICO containing the relevant small and large icon sizes.
- Light-background, dark-background, and monochrome previews.
- A small-size preview sheet showing actual-size taskbar and shortcut usage.

Create an optically simplified small-size version if necessary. Do not rely
only on shrinking the largest artwork. Platform-specific packaging assets can
be derived from the master once desktop packaging targets are finalized.

## Acceptance criteria

- Recognizable at 16 and 32 pixels without reading text.
- Clearly visible against both light and dark surroundings.
- Distinct from generic finance icons.
- Consistent with the product's precise and restrained visual identity.
- Clean edges and intentional padding at every exported size.

## Copy-ready design prompt

Design three distinct desktop app icon concepts for Trade-log Analytics, an
offline Python backtesting and strategy-analysis application used by a trading
team. Express precision, structured analysis, and comparison of test results.
Explore a ledger symbol, a simplified comparison motif, and a custom T or TL
monogram. Use simple geometry, a strong silhouette, dark charcoal, off-white,
and a restrained warm coral accent. Avoid generic rising arrows, currency
symbols, bulls, bears, tiny charts, and neon effects. Show each concept on
light and dark backgrounds and at small taskbar sizes. Treat these as concept
explorations; select a direction before producing final production assets.
